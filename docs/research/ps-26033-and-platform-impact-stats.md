# Verify PS 26033 Primary Source & Platform Impact Statistics

## Problem Statement 26033
**Verified Primary Details:**
- **Problem ID:** SIH26033
- **Title:** Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.
- **Sponsoring Organization:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution (Department of Consumer Affairs)
- **Category:** Software Edition
- **Domain:** Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Goal:** To address economic disparity from long supply chains by creating a digital marketplace or platform connecting farmers/FPOs directly with consumers.
*Source: [SIH 2026 Problem Statement 26033 via Official and Secondary Aggregators]*

## Platform Impact Statistics & Reality Checks

### 1. eNAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market)
- **Registered Users (as of March 2026):** Over 1.80 crore farmers, 2.73 lakh traders, 4,724 FPOs.
- **Trade Volume:** 13.25 crore metric tonnes cumulatively (value ₹4.84 lakh crore).
- **Reality Check / Impact:** While adoption numbers look large, academic reviews (like those by ICRIER and EPW) note that true price integration and farmer realization improvements often lag due to local mandi monopoly resistance and logistical hurdles. Much of the recorded volume is sometimes local trade logged digitally rather than true interstate, disintermediated discovery.
*Sources: Official eNAM data (enam.gov.in), ICRIER, EPW analytical pieces on agricultural market reforms.*

### 2. AgriStack
- **Farmer IDs:** Over 10.31 crore Kisan IDs generated as of August 2026 out of a target of 11 crore by 2026-27.
- **Rollout Pace:** Fast-tracked and linked to Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) and Minimum Support Price (MSP) systems.
- **Documented Gaps & Criticisms:** 
  - **Exclusion Risks:** Tenant farmers and landless laborers are often excluded as IDs require digitized Records of Rights (land ownership).
  - **Data Security:** Concerns regarding data privacy given the MoUs signed with private tech companies.
  - **Digital Divide:** Difficulties for rural populations to onboard due to lack of digital literacy and internet connectivity.
*Sources: Government rollout reports, academic critiques on AgriStack.*

### 3. ONDC Agriculture Network
- **Growth Trend:** Early/Nascent phase. While overall ONDC has massive volumes, the agriculture segment is in the "foundational" or pilot stage.
- **Transaction Volume:** Around 7,000 FPOs onboarded, with tens of thousands of transactions (e.g., ~30,000 by mid-2024), scaling slowly compared to retail.
- **Failure Modes / Reality:** Still building the basic rails for B2B/B2C direct selling. FPOs struggle with logistics, cataloging, and digital fulfillment readiness.
*Sources: ONDC quarterly reports and tech ecosystem analyses.*
