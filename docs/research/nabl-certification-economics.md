# NABL Certification Economics at Smallholder Scale

## 1. NABL Laboratory Network & Density
NABL (National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories) has a wide network of accredited labs in India. However, the density of specialized agricultural/food testing labs is heavily concentrated in urban centers and major agricultural hubs. 
- In major producing states like **Maharashtra** (turmeric), **Rajasthan** (mustard), and **Himachal Pradesh** (honey), access to private NABL labs exists but often requires shipping samples from rural FPOs to state capitals or tier-2 cities, adding logistical costs and time delays (typically 3-7 days turnaround).

## 2. Testing Costs per Sample
The cost for an FPO to get a sample tested at an existing NABL-accredited lab is **not fixed** and depends on the specific parameters (e.g., pesticide residues, heavy metals, adulteration, nutritional profile).
- **Average Cost Range:** Typically ranges between **₹1,000 to ₹5,000+ per sample**.
- For specific crops like Turmeric (curcumin content, adulterants like lead chromate), Mustard (oil content, erucic acid), and Honey (NMR testing for sugar syrup adulteration, antibiotics), comprehensive export-grade tests can lean toward the higher end (₹3,000 - ₹5,000+).

## 3. Subsidies & Financial Support for FPOs
**Does an existing subsidy cover this testing cost for smallholders?**
- **Partial/Conditional Yes:** There is no universal "free testing" scheme for all smallholders. However, there are targeted subsidies:
  1. **Testing Fee Reimbursement:** Under the National SC-ST Hub (NSSH), MSMEs/FPOs owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs can get **80% reimbursement** (up to ₹1,00,000 per year) on testing fees from NABL-accredited labs. 
  2. **Setting up Labs:** For FPOs looking to scale and internalize this cost, the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) under the PMKSY scheme provides a **70% subsidy on equipment** and **100% reimbursement of NABL accreditation costs** (up to ₹5 Crore) for FPOs setting up new testing labs.

**Conclusion for Pitch:** At the individual smallholder scale, ₹1,000-₹5,000 per batch is economically unviable. The BharatPure platform's model must aggregate produce at the FPO/Cluster level. The FPO absorbs the ₹3,000 testing cost for a pooled 500kg-1000kg batch, reducing the per-kg certification cost to a negligible ₹3-₹6/kg. This marginal cost is easily recovered by the premium pricing unlocked by the NABL certificate on the platform.
