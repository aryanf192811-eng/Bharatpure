## Brand & Style

The design system blends agricultural authenticity with intuitive gamification. It balances the credibility demanded by institutional bulk buyers with the high-trust, low-friction accessibility required by rural smallholder farmers and FPOs across India.

### Core Brand Principles
- **Earthy Vitality:** Grounded in rich foliage, fertile soils, and warm harvests. It departs entirely from sterile tech whites and cold corporate blues.
- **Dignified Gamification:** Employs progress rings, streak counters, verification badges, and milestone rewards reminiscent of motivational learning apps, but executed with agrarian symbolism (sprouts, sheaves, soil tiers) and strict financial clarity.
- **Universal Accessibility & Dual-Audience Utility:** Built mobile-first with high tactile feedback, visual redundancy (icons, colors, and text paired at all times), and oversized 48px minimum touch targets to serve varied literacy levels in bright outdoor conditions, while providing granular telemetry for wholesale buyers.

## Colors

The palette derives from agricultural cycles: deep evergreen foliage, ripe mustard harvests, terracotta clay, and rich alluvial silt. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Forest Green):** Represents trust, healthy yields, and stability.
  - `primary-50` (`#F0FDF4`): Subdued card backdrops, active row fills.
  - `primary-100` (`#D8F3DC`): Badge backdrops, completed progress fill.
  - `primary-400` (`#74C69D`): Active borders, secondary iconography.
  - `primary-500` (`#52B788`): Progress bars, positive indicators.
  - `primary-600` (`#40916C`): Standard interactive elements and toggles.
  - `primary-700` (`#2D6A4F`): Default brand primary, headers, primary buttons.
  - `primary-800` (`#1B4332`): High-contrast text, active button states.
  - `primary-900` (`#0D2818`): Deep display headings and grounded borders.
- **Secondary (Harvest Gold):** Motivates streak milestones, premium lot tiers, quality certifications, and escrow guarantees.
  - `gold-50` (`#FFFBEB`), `gold-100` (`#FDF3D0`), `gold-400` (`#E9B949`), `gold-600` (`#C8971A`), `gold-800` (`#92650A`).
- **Tertiary (Terracotta):** High-priority call-to-actions, urgent bids, expiring bulk deals, and verification actions.
  - `terracotta-100` (`#FFEDD5`), `terracotta-400` (`#E8795F`), `terracotta-600` (`#C4522A`), `terracotta-700` (`#9A3412`).
- **Neutral (Earth Soil):** Replaces clinical slate grays with warm, dust-tolerant tones.
  - `earth-50` (`#FDF8F0`): Primary canvas backdrop.
  - `earth-100` (`#F5F0E8`): Card and container surfaces.
  - `earth-200` (`#E2D9CC`): Inactive borders, divider rules.
  - `earth-500` (`#9B8B72`): Secondary metadata labels and auxiliary icons.
  - `earth-700` (`#5C4F38`): Body copy and field legends.
  - `earth-900` (`#1C1408`): High-contrast primary copy.
- **Structural Border & Shadow:**
  - `border-sage`: `#C9DFCE`
  - `shadow-forest`: `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08)`

## Typography

Typography establishes an unambiguous dichotomy between conversational guidance and transactional precision.

- **Display & Interface (Inter):** Chosen for supreme legibility across vernacular script translations, tall x-height, and robust rendering on budget mobile displays under harsh sunlight.
- **Monospaced Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Applied deliberately to all numeric and verification data points—including crop prices per quintal, lot weights, moisture percentages, quality grades, geolocation tags, escrow IDs, and streak scores. This reinforces scientific exactness and builds trust between counterparties.
- All body copy defaults to high contrast against `earth-50` or `earth-100` surfaces to remain legible in high-glare environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, prioritizing one-handed thumb interaction along the lower two-thirds of the viewport.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with a base canvas margin of `1rem` (16px) and gutters of `1rem` (16px). All interactive touchpoints sit within a bottom-anchored action area.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and margins, facilitating side-by-side verification and inspection views.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at `1280px` centered canvas, with `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters to accommodate multi-lot procurement tables.

### Vertical Rhythm
Built upon a foundational 4px/8px incremental grid. Interactive components (inputs, CTA buttons, quick-filter chips) must never sit below `48px` in physical tap height. Stacked form elements use `space-md` (16px) for distinct separation, preventing mis-taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids artificial clinical drop shadows, adopting organic, green-tinted ambient diffusions inspired by natural canopy lighting.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `earth-50` (`#FDF8F0`). Never elevated.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards):** Flat `earth-100` or `#FFFFFF` bound by a structural 1px border of `border-sage` (`#C9DFCE`). Used for default inventory rows, farmer profiles, and milestone boards.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated & Interactive):** Used for pinned trade summaries, active bottom sheets, and floating CTA triggers.
  - *Shadow formula:* `0 4px 16px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.04)`
  - *Border:* `1px solid #C9DFCE`
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Used for deal execution popups and streak celebration dialogs.
  - *Shadow formula:* `0 12px 32px rgba(13, 40, 24, 0.14), 0 2px 6px rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.06)`
- **Gamified "Press" Tactility:** Milestone badges and primary buttons feature a distinct bottom bevel (a subtle 2px solid base border in `primary-800` or `terracotta-700`) giving a punchy, physical, depressible feel when tapped.

## Shapes

Shapes balance the approachable, rewarding roundness of gamification with the structural discipline of financial commerce.

- **Cards & Modals:** Standardized to `rounded-lg` (16px). This eliminates harsh corners without feeling childlike, providing a welcoming container for trade metrics.
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** Standardized to 12px (within the `rounded-lg` paradigm) to keep form edges readable when nested inside 16px parent cards.
- **Gamified Badges & Quick Filters:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to signal tapability, state toggling, and collectible achievements.
- **Data Callouts & QR Tags:** 8px (`rounded-md`) to frame sharp JetBrains Mono data without excessive curvature distorting dense tables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta):** Reserved for core value steps (e.g., "Place Bid", "Accept Mandate", "Confirm Dispatch"). Background `terracotta-600`, text `#FFFFFF`, 48px height, 12px corner radius. Features a 2px bottom bevel in `terracotta-700` that depresses (`transform: translateY(2px)`) on tap.
- **Secondary / Confirm (Forest Green):** Background `primary-700`, text `#FFFFFF`, with a `primary-800` bottom rim for daily routine actions like "Add Harvest Lot".
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Surface `earth-50`, border `1.5px solid border-sage`, text `primary-700`.

### Gamified Streak & Progress Indicators
- **Milestone Circular Rings:** SVG rings using `primary-100` as the track and `primary-500` as the progress stroke. Center displays JetBrains Mono numbers paired with an agricultural icon (e.g., sprout, sheaf, grain bag).
- **Streak Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `gold-100`, border `1px solid gold-400`, text `gold-800`. Always prefixed with a flame or grain icon followed by the streak count in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Cards & Marketplace Lots
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` or `earth-100` with a 1px `border-sage` frame.
- **Header Slot:** Farmer/FPO verified checkmark pill badge on the top right; crop category visual icon on the left.
- **Body Metric Grid:** A 2x2 split utilizing `JetBrains Mono` for quantity (`Quintals`), moisture level (`%`), and baseline ask (`₹/kg`).
- **Footer Action:** Inline Terracotta CTA button alongside a direct call/audio toggle for vernacular assistance.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Container:** Height 52px, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid border-sage`, corner radius 12px.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `primary-600` with a soft outer ring of `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.15)`.
- **Numeric Inputs:** Currency and quantity values render exclusively in `JetBrains Mono` at `data-lg` (20px) weight to avoid misread pricing.

### Selection Chips & Toggles
- **Default State:** Height 40px, pill shape, background `earth-100`, border `1px solid earth-200`, text `earth-700`.
- **Active State:** Background `primary-50`, border `1.5px solid primary-600`, text `primary-800`, accompanied by a trailing checkmark.

### Verification Badges
- **FPO/Quality Certified:** Pill container with `gold-50` fill, `1px solid gold-400` border, `gold-800` text, displaying a shield icon with an embedded verification hash code in `JetBrains Mono`.
