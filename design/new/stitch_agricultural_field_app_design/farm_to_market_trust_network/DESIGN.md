# BharatPure Design System — Farm-to-Market Trust Network

## Brand & Visual Personality
Rooted in authentic Indian agriculture, BharatPure connects rural farming clusters (FPOs) directly with consumers, bulk buyers, and markets. The visual tone balances organic earthy warmth with institutional trust, NABL-grade verification rigor, and high-definition agricultural imagery (golden harvest wheat, lush turmeric roots, deep monsoon fields, warm terracotta earth).

## Color Tokens
- Primary Brand (Forest Green): #1B4332 (Main brand, trust, deep organic vitality)
- Primary Dark: #0D2818
- Primary Mid/Light: #2D6A4F, #52B788, #D8F3DC, #F0FDF4
- Harvest Gold: #C8971A (Main gold, premium grade, NABL verified certification)
- Gold Accents: #92650A, #E9B949, #FDF3D0, #FFFBEB
- Terracotta Accent: #C4522A (Urgent CTAs, earth energy, live auctions)
- Background / Canvas: #FDF8F0 (Warm parchment organic background)
- Surface / Cards: #FFFFFF (Crisp white elevation cards)
- Surface Alt: #F5F0E8 (Off-white / sand for contrast)
- Border / Line: #E2D9CC (Subtle warm grey)
- Text Primary: #1C1408 (Deep ink)
- Text Secondary: #5C4F38 (Earthy medium ink)
- Text Muted: #9B8B72 (Subtle botanical grey)
- Semantic Success: #2D6A4F, Semantic Warning: #E8841A, Semantic Danger: #C4522A

## Typography
- Display / Headings: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif (heritage, craft, premium single-origin produce)
- Body / UI: 'Inter', -apple-system, sans-serif (crisp legibility, high contrast)
- Monospace / Codes: 'JetBrains Mono', monospace (batch codes, NABL lab IDs, tamper-proof hashes)

## Layout & Components
- Shell: Responsive top navigation bar with botanical watermark branding, clear role switchers, and sticky bottom navigation for mobile PWA.
- Cards: Soft organic shadows, subtle borders, high-res agricultural photography headers, clean metric pills, verified green checkpoints.
